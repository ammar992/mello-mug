# Updating Your Website

Sign in at **yourdomain.com/admin**. Everything on the website can be changed here, and changes appear on the website as soon as you save.

## Change a price (10 seconds)

1. Go to **Menu & prices**.
2. Click the price box next to the item, type the new price, then press **Enter** or click anywhere else.
3. "Price updated" appears. Done.

## Add, edit, or remove a menu item

- **Add menu item** (top right): enter the name, price, category, description, and photo, and optional tags like *Vegan* or *Seasonal*.
- **Pencil**: edit everything about an item, including its photo.
- **Star**: *featured*. Featured items show first in the homepage menu.
- **Sparkle**: *best seller*. The item appears in the Best sellers section with a badge.
- **Eye**: show or hide the item. Hiding is good for seasonal drinks.
- **↑ ↓**: change the order. Use this in the "All items" view.
- **Bin**: delete permanently.

**Categories** (left panel): add, rename (pencil), hide (eye), reorder, or delete. A category can only be deleted once it has no items left.

## Change photos

Every image field has **Choose image / Replace** and **Remove**.

- *Choose image* opens your **Media library**. Upload a new photo there by dragging it in, or pick one you've already uploaded.
- Hover over any photo in the library and click the **red bin** to delete it permanently.
- Photos look best at least 1600 px wide. Square or 4:3 works best for menu items, and portrait for team photos.
- Fill in **Alt text**, a short description of the photo. It helps visually impaired visitors and Google.

## Opening hours, address, and map

Go to **Hours, location & footer**:

- **Opening hours**: one row per day range, e.g. "Monday – Friday" with "7:00 AM – 7:00 PM". The website highlights today's row automatically.
- **Address / map**: type your address and the map appears. For an exact pin, paste a Google Maps *embed* link (instructions are under the field).
- **Phone & email**: these power the click-to-call and click-to-email buttons.
- **Currency symbol**: shown before every price ($, £, €, Rs…).
- **Social media** and **Footer** text are here too.

## Homepage text

**Homepage** lets you edit:

- the hero heading and buttons
- the scrolling words band
- the 7 captions of the bean-to-cup animation, plus the final welcome message
- the headings of the Menu, Best sellers, Team, and Visit sections

In the hero heading, press **Enter** to make the second line italic caramel.

## Our story & Team

- **Our story**: heading, introduction, full story, quote, up to 3 photos, and up to 4 "what we care about" cards.
- **Team**: add people with a name, role, short bio, and portrait photo. Hide or reorder them anytime.

## Saving

When you edit a page, a dark bar appears at the bottom. Click **Save changes** (or press `Ctrl + S`), or **Discard** to undo. Menu items, categories, and team members save instantly; they don't use the bar.

## Password

**Settings → Change password.** Changing it signs you out on other devices.
